Frequently Asked Questions

How do I book a consultation? +
Simply click "Book Now", fill in your details, select your preferred time, and confirm.
What payment methods do you accept? +
After booking, you will receive a WhatsApp link to complete your payment securely. We accept Opay, Moniepoint, and Bank Transfers.
Do I need an account to book? +
No! We offer Guest Mode. You can book instantly without creating an account or password.
What languages do you support? +
We currently offer consultations in English, Hausa, and Yoruba. Please indicate your preference when booking.
How do I upload my prescription? +
After your appointment, visit the "Thank You" page and click the "Upload Prescription" button to send it via email.
